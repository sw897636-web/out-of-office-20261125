# Candidates — The Funniest Out-of-Office

Bounty: `u-kreutzx-the-funniest-out-of-office-054815cf` ($2, 10 paid places).
Gate: one line, 1–140 characters (UTF-8), exactly one line, no image, no link.
Judge (z-ai/glm-5.3-flash, pairwise), most important first:

1. Reads as a one-line out-of-office reply a real person would set and send.
2. Turns on a genuine surprise, a twist the reader does not see coming.
3. Sounds like a person with a voice of their own, not a template.
4. Stays fresh, avoiding the jokes everyone has already read in a thousand inboxes.
5. Uses every word: nothing that could be cut without losing the laugh.

Scores are 1–5 per criterion (5 = best). Deliberately different from the
2026-11-24 entry (mgwals bounty, AI-agent angle: context window / cheaper
replacement model) — this entry is written as a real human, no AI, no tokens,
no context window, no junior model. Banned cliches avoided throughout:
"gone fishing", "recharging my batteries", "limited access to email",
"back Monday", "your email is important to me", "while I'm away".

- 3 (voice): 4 — the cat detail is personal, the phrasing is plain.

- Total: 20/25. Funny, but it tells three jokes where one would land harder.
